01 — The cost

The first hours decide who is found.

The February 2023 earthquake killed more than 53,000 people in Türkiye alone. Most survivors are pulled out in the first three days, and the rescue there was slowed by broken communications and poor coordination. That gap is what RescueNet works on.

Sources: Türkiye Interior Ministry via AP; UCL disaster researcher via NBC News

02 — The weak point

One coordinator is one point of failure.

A central dispatcher needs every radio link to stay up. When the links fail, the teams stand idle and wait for orders that cannot arrive.

03 — The idea

So every team carries the plan.

Each team bids for the incidents it can reach soonest, and the teams agree on the result between themselves (consensus-based bundle allocation). No server and no dispatcher, and every unit computes the same answer.

04 — The watchdog

A failing team is flagged before it stops.

Peers watch each other's radio beacons. When three agree that a team is in trouble, its work is handed to someone else. A small neural network predicts faults, and a watchdog and a digital twin check it.

Finding your port

macOS
Usually /dev/tty.usbmodem…. In Terminal: ls /dev/tty.usb*
Linux
Usually /dev/ttyACM0. You may need to be in the dialout group.
Windows
A COM number. Device Manager → Ports (COM & LPT).

Boards with an ST-LINK show up as a virtual COM port once they are plugged in over USB.
