Who hears whom

Every team listens for the others' radio beacons and judges them by what it hears, with no central computer deciding.

A line is two teams hearing each other on time; a dashed amber line is a beacon that was missed.

A ring round a team is what the others concluded about it: thin when one team is unsure, thick once three or more agree that something is wrong. Hover a team to bring its own links forward.
